## 2. The REAL problems (confirmed)

1. **Miscalibrated gate:** it read the *in-sample full-history* exp_R, inflated 2-3x (TRX
   break_retest gate **+1.23** vs real OOS **+0.39**). -> **FIX:** the gate now measures the
   backtest only on the OOS window `[2025-01, inception)` (`config.gate_backtest_from_ms`),
   and requires a real margin, `gate_min_exp_r` 0.0 -> **+0.05** (covers costs with room).
2. **Small edge (+0.1-0.2 R) and low frequency** (8-25 trades/combo in 5 months):
   confirming live needs *hundreds* of trades. -> **FIX:** grow n by diversifying into
   more winning combos.
3. **Combos without edge in the portfolio:** XRP/DOGE vwap negative in both regimes. ->
   **pruned.**

long-only strategy on a trending asset wins by beta, not alpha). The new tool
`research/validate_alts.py` adds an **anti-beta check** to the double OOS (long/short split
+ the asset's buy & hold): a real edge pays on the SHORT side or with the asset flat/
falling, not just by riding a rally.
