# Final audit v1.0 - 2026-08-03

**Project go/no-go verdict: THERE IS NO EDGE. Project closed.**

This is the closing audit of the freeze declared in v0.9 (2026-07-02). Single source of
truth: `/opt/oscilion/data/oscilion.db` on the Oracle VM, copied on 2026-08-03 16:03 UTC.
The service was healthy for the whole window (75 days of uptime, `oscilion` and
`oscilion-api` active, 0 errors in `events`).

**Sample:** 110 closed trades, 2026-06-08 -> 2026-08-02 (7.8 weeks), `dry-run` mode,
26 active symbol x strategy combos.

---

## 1. Overall result

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Cumulative R | **-64.2 R** |
| Win rate | **14.5%** (breakeven required given the realized RR: **31.4%**) |
| Peak equity | **+0.3 R** (at trade #1) |
| MaxDD | **-64.4 R** (monotonically falling curve) |
| Worst streak | **18 consecutive losers** |
| Exits | 88 `stop` - 8 `tp` - 9 `timeout` |
| Mean R per exit | stop -1.068 - tp +2.379 - timeout +1.653 |

**Monte Carlo (20,000 simulations, observed sd = 1.25R, n = 110):**

| Hypothesis | P(observing <= -64.2R) |
|---|---|
| true expR = **+0.30** (design target) | **0.0e+00** (0 of 20,000) |
| true expR = **0.00** (breakeven) | **< 1e-4** |

It is not variance. A merely breakeven system does not produce this result.

**Dissection notes:**

- **`ema_trend_stack`: 16 out of 16 stops, sd = 0.03.** Zero winners; no trade came even
  partially close to the TP. It is the cleanest possible refutation.
- **`orb_breakout`: the average winner pays only 0.72R.** The RR >= 2.5 required by the
  entry invariant **never materializes**: winners exit on `timeout` with crumbs while
  losers pay the full -1.07R. The implied breakeven win rate rises to **58%** against a
  realized 21% (p = 2.3e-4). It is structurally broken, not a calibration issue.
- **`vwap_anchor`**: 16% win rate against a 29.6% breakeven (p = 0.026). The regime filter
  introduced in v0.8 did not save it (see section 5).
- **`break_retest`** survives the test only thanks to its huge variance (sd 1.97, an
  isolated +4.7R). Its 95% CI crosses zero. The only combo with a real positive sign and
  n >= 4: **HBAR/USDT break_retest +0.38R with n=4**, an anecdote, not evidence.

## 3. Central finding: the backtest does not predict the forward

Spearman correlation between the backtest `exp_r` (`forward_results` table) and the real
forward expR (`trades` table), per symbol x strategy combo:

| Backtest window | n combos | Spearman | p |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `backtest` (full history) | 26 | **-0.216** | 0.29 |
| `oos_a` (>2025) | 22 | **-0.042** | 0.85 |
| `oos_b` (2026) | 23 | **-0.181** | 0.41 |

> **22 combos had a positive expR in the 2026 OOS window. Only 2 were positive in the
> forward test (9%), both with n <= 4. The 22 together: -65.1R over 102 trades.**

Examples of the backtest -> forward collapse:

| Combo | expR OOS-2026 | forward expR | fw n |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| NEO break_retest | +1.298 | -1.060 | 4 |
| RUNE break_retest | +1.282 | -0.282 | 5 |
| TIA vwap_anchor | +1.277 | -0.602 | 8 |
| XAU momentum_pullback | +1.067 | -1.236 | 2 |
| TRX ema_trend_stack | +1.043 | -1.099 | 3 |
| BTC ema_trend_stack | +0.411 | -1.070 | 7 |

**Interpretation:** if the selection pipeline had any predictive power, we would expect a
positive correlation and >= 50% sign agreement. We got zero/negative correlation and 9%.
**All the validation machinery built in June (double OOS, anti-beta, purged walk-forward,
adaptive gate, clusters, Kelly sizing) was optimizing over a signal that does not exist.**
The problem is not the five strategies: it is the method that selected them.

Operational corollary: **any future strategy validated with this pipeline will repeat this
result.** Fixing the method is a prerequisite for any continuation.

## 4. There was no market excuse

BTC/USDT during the forward window (weekly closes):

| Week | Close |
|---|---:|
| 2026-06-14 | 65 702 |
| 2026-06-28 | 59 550 |
| 2026-07-12 | 63 750 |
| 2026-07-26 | 65 375 |
| 2026-08-02 | 63 550 |

**Net change over 8 weeks: +0.4%. Range 58k-67k.** This is exactly the **sideways regime**
the project's thesis (`VISION.md`: reversion inside ranges) claims to exploit. The system
lost -64R in its theoretically most favorable environment.

This retroactively invalidates the excuse that saved the project in the 2026-06-22 audit
("it was not overfit, it was a hostile fortnight over a small edge"). With four times more
sample and a benign regime, the result got worse.

## 5. The validation design could never converge

- 26 active combos, 14.0 trades/week in total -> **0.54 trades/week per combo**.
- Observed sd = 1.25R.

| Edge to detect | n required (80% power) | Time per combo |
|---|---:|---:|
| expR = +0.30 | 136 trades | **4.9 years** |
| expR = +0.50 | 49 trades | **1.7 years** |

The v0.9 rule ("freeze until ~100 capital trades") was unworkable at symbol x strategy
granularity: no individual combo would have reached significance this decade. What
**did** converge, and decisively, is the aggregate of the 110 observations.

---

## 6. Costs: honest and minor

The mean realized stop is **-1.068R** => friction ~ **0.068R per trade** ~ **-7.5R** of the
total -64.2R (**12% of the damage**). The v0.8 `max_cost_r = 0.12` filter works. Costs are
not the cause of the loss; the strategies lose on their own.

---

## 7. Integrity residue (does not change the verdict)

1. **44 pre-v0.9 trades with `exit_ts < ts`** (~-15 min): known residue of the `ts` bug
   fixed in v0.9. The `r_multiple` is correct; only the time analysis of those rows needs
   to use `exit_ts`.
2. **19 `break_retest` trades with `tp` = NaN or 1e18**: that family exits by
   trailing/timeout, not by a fixed TP. It explains its high sd. It does not invalidate
   the R.

Neither affects the conclusions: the verdict rests on `r_multiple`, which is audited
(`cost_audit` populated, stops at exactly -1.0 gross).

## 9. Project balance

The stated goal was to **validate whether a profitable trading bot could be built**. With
110 real trades, in the market regime most favorable to the thesis, and with honest cost
accounting, the measured answer is **NO**.

That is a valid result, and a cheap one: it was paid in paper R (`dry-run`), never in
capital. The honesty invariant in `VISION.md` ("the backtest may say *there is no edge*;
that is accepted") holds here in its strongest form: the **forward** said it, and it is
accepted.

**Project closed at v1.0.**
